# Business Contact Extractor

Everything you need to install and use **Business Contact Extractor by Blaster Pro Tools**.

Business Contact Extractor is a developer-mode Google Chrome extension that helps you collect publicly available business names, WhatsApp and phone numbers, emails, websites, and source links from Google search results. Selected websites can then be scanned for missing or additional public contact details.

## Quick start

1. Download the Business Contact Extractor ZIP file and keep your licence key ready.
2. Extract it into a permanent folder named `Business Contact Extractor`.
3. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable **Developer mode**, and choose **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the `Business Contact Extractor` folder that directly contains `manifest.json`.
5. Pin Extractor and activate the licence from its popup.
6. Choose the correct country and calling code before collecting phone numbers.
7. Search Google normally or use a template from the [Search Query Handbook](guides/search-queries/README.md).
8. Collect the visible Google results, continue through more pages, and review the combined list.
9. Scan selected business websites when you need missing or additional public contact details.
10. Export the finished list to CSV or JSON.

## Responsible use

Only collect information that businesses have made public. Follow applicable privacy, marketing, anti-spam, platform, and telecommunications rules. Verify contact relevance and consent requirements before outreach. Do not use the extension to access private, restricted, login-protected, or non-public information.
