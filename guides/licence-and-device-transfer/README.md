# Licence Activation and Device Transfer

This guide explains how Business Contact Extractor activation works and what to do when you want to use the licence in another Chrome profile, browser installation, or computer.

## What the licence covers

- A licence is activated for one Extractor installation and Chrome profile at a time.
- Internet access is required for activation and periodic subscription validation.
- Your name, phone number, email, and city may be requested during first activation so the licence can be connected to the correct customer.
- The extension stores its activation details inside the active Chrome profile.
- A normal extension update should not require a licence transfer when you keep the same `Business Contact Extractor` folder and Chrome profile.

## When a licence reset is required

Ask Blaster Pro Tools to reset the licence before activating it in any of these situations:

- You are moving to another computer.
- You are moving to another Chrome profile.
- You removed the extension and the new installation is treated as a different installation.
- Chrome was reinstalled or its profile data was cleared.
- Activation says the licence is already active on another machine or installation.

The reset removes the old installation binding. It does not extend or change the subscription expiration date.

## Move Extractor safely

1. Open the Extractor dashboard on the old installation.
2. Export important contacts to CSV or JSON.
3. Keep a copy of your customer ZIP and licence key.
4. Contact Blaster Pro Tools and request a licence reset.
5. Install Extractor in the destination Chrome profile by following the [Installation Guide](../installation/README.md).
6. Open the extension and activate the same licence key with your customer details.
7. Confirm the subscription status before collecting contacts.

## Updating on the same computer

For a normal update, keep the permanent folder named `Business Contact Extractor` and replace its extension files with the new release. Then open `chrome://extensions` and select the extension's reload button. Do not rename or delete the folder, and do not remove the extension unless the update instructions specifically tell you to do so.

## Common licence messages

| Message | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Licence required | No saved licence is available | Open the popup and activate your key |
| Invalid licence key | The server could not find the key | Check every character and contact support if it continues |
| Platform mismatch | The key was issued for another product | Ask support to verify the product connected to the key |
| Already activated elsewhere | The key is bound to another installation | Request a licence reset before trying again |
| Licence expired | The subscription end date has passed | Renew the subscription |
| Could not connect | The licence service could not be reached | Check internet access, firewall settings, date and time, then retry |

## Before requesting a reset

Send support your licence key, purchase email, current computer operating system, and a short explanation of why you are moving. Never send passwords, browser cookies, payment card details, or remote-access credentials.
