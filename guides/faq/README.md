# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Business Contact Extractor?

It is a Google Chrome extension that collects publicly available business contact details from visible Google search results and can scan selected public business websites for missing or additional details.

## Which systems are supported?

The extension is designed for desktop Google Chrome on Windows and macOS.

## Does it work on Android or iPhone?

No. Mobile Chrome does not provide the same desktop extension environment required by this product.

## Is it installed from the Chrome Web Store?

No. Customers receive a developer-mode package with installation instructions and licence activation.

## Is internet access required?

Yes. Google searches, website scanning, activation, and periodic subscription validation require internet access.

## How many computers can use one licence?

The standard licence supports one active Extractor installation and Chrome profile at a time unless your purchase states otherwise.

## Can I move the licence?

Yes. Export your contacts, ask Blaster Pro Tools to reset the old binding, then activate the same valid licence on the new installation. See [Licence Activation and Device Transfer](../licence-and-device-transfer/README.md).

## Will an update erase my activation?

A normal update should keep it when the same `Business Contact Extractor` folder and Chrome profile are preserved. Removing the extension, changing profiles, or clearing browser data can create a new installation identity.

## Will uninstalling erase my saved contacts?

Chrome may remove the extension's local storage when the extension is uninstalled. Export important contacts before removing or reinstalling it.

## What information can it collect?

Depending on what is publicly visible, records may contain a business name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, website, source domain, source URL, search query, confidence, and collection date.

## Does every Google result produce a contact?

No. A result is only useful when recognizable public contact information is present in the visible result content or its public links.

## Why are some visible numbers rejected?

Numbers are validated against the selected country and calling code. Dates, IDs, short fragments, impossible numbers, wrong-country numbers, and duplicates should be rejected.

## Why do I need to choose a country?

Country selection lets Extractor interpret local phone formats and reject unrelated numbers more accurately.

## How are emails categorized?

Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, Hotmail, Live, and related providers are categorized as personal-provider emails. Addresses using a company domain are categorized as business emails.

## Does the website scanner work on every site?

No. Login pages, CAPTCHAs, image-only contacts, JavaScript-only content, security services, and site access rules can prevent scanning. It does not bypass protections.

## What does website permission allow?

It allows the extension to request the public website pages that you choose to scan. It does not give the extension permission to discover passwords or access private accounts.

## Are duplicate contacts removed?

Extractor merges repeated contacts automatically using the contact information available in each record. You should still review the finished list because businesses can publish several legitimate numbers or emails.

## Which export formats are available?

The dashboard can export the visible contact list to CSV or JSON. CSV is convenient for spreadsheets and CRM imports. JSON preserves structured record fields.

## Do I need advanced Google operators?

No. Normal searches such as `boutiques in Dubai` can work. Operators help narrow noisy results when you need a particular source, email pattern, location, or exclusion. See the [Search Query Handbook](../search-queries/README.md).

## Can I contact every collected business?

Collection does not automatically create permission to send marketing. Use relevant public business contacts responsibly and follow applicable privacy, anti-spam, marketing, and telecommunications rules.
