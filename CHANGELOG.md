# Changelog

Customer-facing release notes for Business Contact Extractor.

## 1.5.0

- Improved strict country-based phone number validation.
- Added clearer country and calling-code handling for Google collection and website scanning.
- Improved rejection of short, impossible, wrong-country, and joined number fragments.
- Expanded email cleaning for malformed text, joined labels, copied snippets, and common provider-domain suffixes.
- Improved email categorization for business domains, Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, Hotmail, Live, and related providers.
- Improved website scanning for public contact, about, support, help, phone, email, and WhatsApp links.
- Improved merging of additional website contacts into existing Google records.
- Continued automatic duplicate handling across collected records.
- Retained CSV and JSON export from the contact dashboard.
- Updated the installation experience around the permanent `Business Contact Extractor` folder.
- Expanded installation, search-query, licence-transfer, scanner, troubleshooting, and FAQ guidance.
