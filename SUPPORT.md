# Customer Support

Business Contact Extractor support is provided by Blaster Pro Tools.

## Contact

[Visit Blaster Pro Tools](https://blasterprotools.com/)

Use the website contact option for licence activation, licence resets, installation problems, billing questions, and product support.

## Include these details

To help us reproduce the problem, include:

- your purchase email;
- the licence key, if the issue is related to activation;
- Windows or macOS version;
- Google Chrome version;
- Extractor version;
- the exact error message;
- a screenshot with private information hidden;
- the action you were performing when the problem occurred; and
- one public example search query or website URL, when relevant.

## Never send

Do not send your password, browser cookies, one-time codes, complete payment card details, private customer databases, or remote-access credentials.

## Before contacting support

1. Read the [Installation Guide](guides/installation/README.md).
2. Check [Troubleshooting](guides/troubleshooting/README.md).
3. Restart Chrome and retry once.
4. Confirm your system date, time, and internet connection.
5. Confirm you are using the latest official Business Contact Extractor files from Blaster Pro Tools.

## Licence transfer requests

State that you want a licence reset and explain whether you are changing computer, Chrome profile, or reinstalling the extension. Export saved contacts before removing the old installation.
